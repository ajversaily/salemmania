Issue No. 1 · Founder's Note · Ohio, March 2026
The Mania Begins.

Every documented cultural shift has occurred after a change has come. During the Salem Witch Trials, the hysteria only ended when eyes shifted from the accused toward the persecutors. The court was questioned. Salem Mania is here to document the next shift — across film, music, culture, literature, opinion, and poetry.
