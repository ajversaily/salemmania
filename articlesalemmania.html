THE MANIA IS GOING CRAZY FOR COMMUNITY!

Salem Mania is an independent writing blog. It does not have the incentive to profit, and the code is open source. Meaning those who want to get involved are allowed to do so! and it is in fact highly encouraged by the founder! This is Salem Mania's official first article, and I thought it’d be best for the first article to be a kind of ‘log’ on what my mission is, the purpose of Salem Mania going forward and some things I want to explain.

WHAT IS SALEM MANIA? As I already established before, Salem Mania is an independent writing blog. A passion project if you will. Salem Mania does not create any profit but is open to donations and contributions. Salem Mania will be heavily peer reviewed by those involved. The Mania will cover a wide range of topics from writers. From film analysis, music reviews, poems, literary arguments, opinions, and culture. Basically anything that is worth writing about! The Mania is a place by independents for independents, without the monitoring of social media nor the profit drive of corporations, the people are free to write what they want and get involved! The web is open, and I weave my first thread! Thank you! Cordially, ARAS
